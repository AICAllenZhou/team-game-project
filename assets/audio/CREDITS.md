# Gunshot recording

Source: “Gunshots” by kurt, OpenGameArt (2017).
https://opengameart.org/content/gunshots
Original: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Black%20Powder.wav
License: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

revolver-1.wav, revolver-2.wav and revolver-3.wav are individual excerpts
of the black-powder recording, trimmed to 0.56 seconds, filtered to reduce
background noise, faded and converted to 48 kHz stereo PCM.
The source does not identify a specific revolver model.
